The moment

From impossible,
to inevitable.

For decades the world believed planting a billion trees in a single day was impossible. Ethiopia just proved otherwise. Now Kenya is leading 12 nations to cross the billion mark together on Earth Day, 22 April 2027, and to keep doing it, every Earth Day, until it sticks.

2019

Ethiopia plants 354M trees in a single day, mobilising 23M citizens.

2024World record

Treegens founder Jimi Cohen personally planted 30,469 mangroves in 24 hours.

Certified 22 April 2024 · Kwale, Kenya

2025Ethiopia Green Legacy

714.7 million trees planted in a single day, with 29 million participants mobilised nationwide.

31 July 2025 · Green Legacy Initiative

2026Kenya pioneers

Kenya's Scouts Association (4.4 million members) joins the GROWalition. Rotary and Truphena are also on board for Kenya's country-wide tree planting day.

2027

Kenya leads the first global billion-tree day on Earth Day, 22 April. 11 other countries have already committed, with firm pledges to be signed at COP31.

Government backed

Endorsed at the highest level.

Sitting Environment Ministers and national programme leads have committed personally, face to face and on the record, at COP29 and COP30. Firm commitments from 12 nations will be signed at COP31, with Kenya leading the charge.
