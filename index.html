COP31 · Antalya, Türkiye · 19 November 2026

Eleven nations pledge to plant one billion trees in a single day.

At COP31, ten governments joined Kenya in the GROWalition, which will attempt the world's first billion-tree day on Earth Day, 22 April 2027. There has never been one billion trees planted in a single day.

How it started

From impossible,
to inevitable.

The GROWalition began in Kenya, where climate champion Truphena Muthoni teamed up with Australian environmentalist Jimi Cohen. Kenya is leading the way, and the Kenya Scouts Association has pledged its 4.4 million members to plant on the day.

72 hrsTruphena Muthoni

Truphena held on to a tree for 72 hours straight, the world record for the longest marathon hugging a tree (her previous record was 48 hours, set in February 2025). She is an Ambassador for Kenya's campaign to grow 15 billion trees by 2032.

Climate champion · Kenya

56,018Jimi Cohen

Jimi, founder of Treegens, has broken three world records for tree planting. Over Earth Day 2024 he planted 30,469 mangroves in 24 hours in Kwale County, Kenya. On 2 to 3 November 2026 he planted 56,018 mangroves in 24 hours at Shimoni, Kenya, a result now under official review.

Founder and CEO, Treegens · Australia

The road to a billion

  1. 2019

    Ethiopia plants 354M trees in a single day, mobilising 23M citizens.

  2. April 2024

    Jimi Cohen plants 30,469 mangroves in 24 hours in Kwale County, Kenya, a world record.

  3. 31 July 2025

    Ethiopia's Green Legacy Initiative plants 714.7 million trees in a single day, with 29 million participants.

  4. January 2026

    Truphena Muthoni hugs a tree for 72 hours, the world record for the longest marathon hugging a tree.

  5. 26 July 2026

    Truphena and Jimi join the Kenya Scouts Association at Jomvu, Mombasa, where 4,124 people set the world record for the most people planting mangrove trees simultaneously.

  6. 3 August 2026

    Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed announces that more than 26 million Ethiopians planted 805.3 million seedlings in a single day, an unofficial world record that inspired the GROWalition's goal.

  7. 2 to 3 November 2026

    Jimi Cohen plants 56,018 mangroves in 24 hours at Shimoni, Kenya. The result is under official review.

  8. 19 November 2026

    At COP31 in Antalya, ten governments join Kenya. Eleven nations pledge to plant together.

  9. 22 April 2027

    Earth Day: eleven nations plant within the same 24 hours, aiming for more than one billion trees. The day comes ahead of COP32, which Ethiopia hosts in Addis Ababa in 2027.

Why one day matters

The four-minute mile, but for the planet.

For decades, many believed no one could run a mile in under four minutes. Then, on 6 May 1954, Roger Bannister ran 3:59.4 in Oxford. Just 46 days later, Australia's John Landy ran 3:57.9 in Turku, Finland. More than 2,000 athletes have now broken the barrier, including 35 American high school students.

"Nothing physical changed in 1954. What changed was belief. Once people see that something is possible, it keeps on happening. The first billion-tree day will be our four-minute mile. It will lead to many more billion-tree days, and with that momentum we can regenerate the world."

Jimi CohenFounder and CEO, Treegens

"I held that tree for 72 hours to remind people what trees mean to us. Now Kenya is inviting the world to plant with us. Every one of those billion seedlings will need someone to care for it after the day is over, and that is where the real work begins."

Truphena MuthoniClimate champion, Kenya
Take action · Earth Day

One day. Four ways in.

Between now and Earth Day, the GROWalition will work with each government on seedlings, planting sites and volunteers, so that every nation plants within the same 24 hours on 22 April 2027. More governments, cities, companies, schools and community groups are invited to join. Pick the role that fits you, and we'll send you exactly what you need to show up on April 22nd.

About the GROWalition

The GROWalition is a coalition of governments and grassroots partners working to plant more than one billion trees in a single day on Earth Day, 22 April 2027. It began in Kenya with climate champion Truphena Muthoni and Jimi Cohen, and is convened by Treegens.
